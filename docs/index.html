Privacy

Your voice never leaves this Mac.

Whisper large-v3 Turbo runs on the Apple Neural Engine. Audio lives in memory, becomes text locally, and is discarded.

  • On-device Recognition runs on the Neural Engine.
  • Nothing uploaded No account, no cloud, no telemetry.
  • One download The speech model is the only network request. Ever.

632 MB, downloaded once. After that, Hush works with Wi-Fi off.

Everywhere

Dictate anywhere you can type.

Slack, Mail, Cursor, a terminal, a browser. Hush pastes at the focused cursor, so every text field becomes a microphone.

  • Any text field If you can type there, you can dictate there.
  • At the cursor The text lands exactly where you were already writing.
  • No window switching Hush lives in the menu bar, not in your way.

Speed

Fast, then gone.

The model loads in under two seconds, uses about one browser tab of memory, and unloads itself when you stop.

  • 1.8 s Cold model load.
  • 130–200 MB RAM while dictating.
  • 8 min Then the model unloads.

Control

Your shortcut. Your rules.

Hold Fn, hold Right Option, use a combination, or record any key. Choose hold-to-talk or hands-free.

  • Any key Fn, a modifier, a combo, or a key you record.
  • Hold or toggle Press and hold, or tap to start and stop.
  • Quiet feedback Four start sounds and one soft stop tick.

Install

Ready in four steps.

The app is small. The speech model is the only big download.

  1. 01

    Download the DMG

    Open it and drag Hush into Applications.

  2. 02

    Allow the microphone

    Hush needs to hear you. Audio is processed in memory.

  3. 03

    Allow Accessibility

    For the global shortcut and pasting into other apps.

  4. 04

    Download the model once

    632 MB, then transcription runs offline.
